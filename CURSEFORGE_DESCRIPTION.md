# 🚣 Alexi's RowBoat

**A fully functional rowboat with working animations and multi-seat support for Hytale!**

## 📖 Description

Alexi's RowBoat brings immersive water transportation to Hytale with beautifully animated rowboats that you can share with friends. Watch the oars move as you paddle across lakes and rivers, with smooth animations that respond to your movement speed and direction.

## ✨ Features

- **🎬 Dynamic Rowing Animations** - Oars move realistically as you paddle forward or reverse
- **👥 Multi-Seat Support** - Take friends along for the ride! Press F to cycle between seats
- **🎮 Intuitive Controls** - Use WASD to navigate, just like you'd expect
- **💧 Water Physics** - Boats float naturally on water surfaces
- **🎯 Easy to Use** - Spawn boats with items or commands

## 🎮 How to Use

1. **Get a rowboat spawn item** from the creative inventory (Vehicles category)
2. **Right-click on water** to place your boat
3. **Press F** to mount the boat
4. **Use W/S** to row forward and backward
5. **Look left/right** to steer

### Commands
```
/hv spawn hyvehicles:simple_boat - Spawn a rowboat
/hv list - View all available vehicles
/hv help - Show all commands
```
